Progress Evolved Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Progress Evolved is a local-first progress tracker. What you record is stored on your phone. There are no accounts, Progress Evolved has no servers of its own, and the app never sends your tracker history, photos, notes, Health Connect data, or backups to us. They leave the phone only when you choose to export, share, or back them up to a place you pick.

The app uses a few Google services: Google AdMob for the banner ad, Google Play Billing for purchases, Google Play for app updates, and Google ML Kit for on-device photo analysis. These services can receive device and usage information, described below. They do not receive tracker history, progress photos, notes, journals, backup contents, or Health Connect records from Progress Evolved.

What the app stores

Progress Evolved stores what you add: projects and the names of the people, pets, or things you track in them; weight, body fat, body measurements, steps, and custom metrics; progress photos with their captions, crops, and blur areas; notes, journals, goals, phases, and chart events; reminders; a record of files you import; and whether you have bought remove-ads. Your profile can also hold your height, used for BMI, and, if you choose, your sex (see Photos).

Tracker data is kept in an encrypted database. Progress photos and their thumbnails are stored encrypted on the device and hidden from the phone gallery. The encryption keys are protected by the Android Keystore on this phone. App preferences, such as your profile, reminder times, album choices, and Health Connect settings, are kept in the app's private storage outside the encrypted database.

Android's own cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer do not copy any Progress Evolved data. To move to a new phone, create a .pebackup backup and restore it there.

Pregnancy projects on Android are private photo journals. The app does not calculate due dates, label pregnancy stages, track symptoms, or give pregnancy health advice. Dates saved by earlier versions can remain in the project and in backups.

Photos

The app can access photos when you take a progress photo, import selected images, share images into Progress Evolved through the Android share sheet, or enable album watch. Album watch uses Android photo-library permission to find new images in the album you choose. You can disable album import in Settings. For a photo imported from the currently watched album, Android 11 and newer may also offer a separate choice to delete the original. Android displays its own confirmation before that source image is deleted; cancelling leaves the Progress Evolved copy intact.

Each photo is kept as it was imported, so details the camera saved with it, such as when it was taken and, if location tagging was on, where, can remain inside the encrypted photo and inside complete backups. Exported photos are redrawn or cleaned and do not carry these details. Photos are never sent to AdMob, Google Play Billing, or us.

On-device photo analysis uses Google ML Kit. It finds faces and body positions, which the app uses to line up and frame comparisons (on by default), crop to the body, blur faces in exports, and suggest private areas. It also reads a scale's display from a photo you take or pick; that photo is not kept, only the weight you save. Photo, image, and recognised text content processed by ML Kit stays on the device and is not sent to Google. Google ML Kit may collect device and app information, performance and usage diagnostics, and per-install identifiers. Other photo checks, such as spotting duplicates and checking brightness, also run on the phone.

Optional private-area detection runs on the device and uploads nothing, using a detection model included with the app. If you set your sex in Settings, detection uses it only to skip body areas that cannot apply to you. Detector results are stored only as pending suggestions and do not change your photos. When detection is on and you export, any photo not yet checked is checked first, suggested areas start blurred in the export review, and you choose what stays blurred. Manual blur remains available because automatic detection can miss areas.

Samsung Secure Folder uses a separate Android profile. Progress Evolved cannot directly read photos across that profile boundary; you can share photos from inside Secure Folder into Progress Evolved through the Android share sheet.

Health Connect

With your permission, Progress Evolved can read weight, body fat, steps, height, and historical Health Connect data, including readings more than 30 days old. You choose how far back to import. Height fills in your profile for BMI. Automatic sync is off by default; if you turn it on, the app imports new readings when you open it.

Optional write-back, off by default, can write weight, body fat, and steps that you log in Progress Evolved to Health Connect. Once written, those records belong to Health Connect: other apps you allow can read them, and deleting entries or data in Progress Evolved does not remove them. You can delete them in Health Connect.

If you delete a weight that came from Health Connect, the app remembers its date and value with your preferences, so a later sync does not bring it back.

Health Connect data is used only to record and show your progress in the app. It is stored locally, is never used for advertising, and is not shared with AdMob, Google Play Billing, or anyone else. It leaves the phone only in exports or backups you make. You can revoke Health Connect permissions in Android settings.

On Samsung devices, Health Connect data inside Secure Folder is isolated from apps outside that profile; Progress Evolved and Health Connect must be in the same Android profile for import or write-back to work.

Bluetooth scales

When you choose to read a Bluetooth scale while adding an entry, the app scans for nearby Bluetooth devices for up to 12 seconds and can connect to a supported scale to read its weight. The names and addresses of nearby devices are used only during that reading and are not saved; only the weight you save is kept. On Android 12 and newer, the app's Bluetooth permissions do not include location access. Android 11 and older require location permission for Bluetooth scanning; Progress Evolved does not read or store your location.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Progress Evolved can show a daily reminder at the time you set, a check-in nudge when a project has gone longer than usual without a log, and notices such as a backup being written. Reminders and nudges name the project, and nudges say how many days it has been since its last log. Depending on your lock-screen settings, they can appear on the lock screen. Nudges are not shown for locked projects. Notifications are scheduled on the phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in Android settings.

App lock

App lock and project locks are optional. PINs and recovery keys are stored only as one-way hashes in Android's secure storage. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Progress Evolved only learns whether it succeeded and never receives biometric data. While a lock is on, the app hides its screen in the recent-apps view. Locks apply to this phone only and are not included in backups.

Exports and backups

You can export CSV files, photo ZIP bundles, PDF summaries, report cards, comparison images and videos, time-lapse videos, single photos, and password-encrypted .pebackup backups. Manual exports are created only when you choose to create them. Only .pebackup backups are encrypted. Other exports are ordinary files that can include your measurements, dates, notes, captions, or project name, listed in the file or drawn onto images, depending on your export settings.

Sharing hands the file to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. Saving lets you choose a location in Android's save dialog, except comparison images and videos, single photos, and time-lapse videos, which are saved to your phone's gallery in DCIM/Progress Evolved Exports (Android 9 and older asks where to save them). Files in the gallery or a shared folder can be seen by other apps with photo or file access, and a photo-backup or cloud-storage app may upload them under its own settings. Copy buttons place text, such as your progress recap, on the phone's clipboard.

Comparison exports show the Progress Evolved mark and a QR code by default. The QR code opens our public tester page and contains nothing about you. You can turn either off in the export options.

Complete .pebackup backups hold every project, items in Trash, your preferences including profile height and sex, and your original photos with their camera details. Data-only backups leave photos out. Before a manual backup, the app asks for the PIN of each locked project that is not already unlocked. Backups do not include app or project locks, so anyone with a backup file and its password can open everything in it. Backup passwords are required to restore backup contents, and the app cannot recover a forgotten password.

Automatic backups are optional. If you enable automatic backups, the app stores your chosen password in device-local secure storage and creates encrypted backups in the device folder you selected. They include locked projects without asking for their PINs. A backup is prepared while the app is open and may finish writing just after you leave it, with a "Writing backup" notification. The app keeps the newest one to three automatic backups, as you choose, and deletes its older automatic backups from that folder. A cloud-storage app may sync that folder under its own settings.

When you import a CSV or spreadsheet file or restore a backup, the app reads only the file you pick, on the phone. It keeps a record of each import: the file name, the columns you matched, and the number of rows.

Ads and purchases

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob until you buy remove-ads. Every banner request asks for non-personalized advertising and limits creatives to Google's general-audience rating. The app does not request access to the Android advertising ID or Android Privacy Sandbox ad-targeting permissions. Before ads start, Google's consent message asks for advertising privacy choices where required; you can review or change them later through Privacy and cookie settings in the app. Google may still process IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and app-scoped device or account identifiers for ad delivery, aggregated reporting, analytics, and fraud prevention. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can remove the banner with a one-time remove-ads purchase. Optional repeatable support purchases grant no features or ad-removal benefit.

Purchases are processed directly by Google Play Billing, which handles the payment flow and store purchase record; Progress Evolved never receives card or payment account details. When the app starts, it asks Google Play which purchases you own. The app processes the Google Play product ID, purchase state, and purchase token locally only as needed to finish purchases and restore remove-ads ownership. It does not send purchase data to us or another billing provider. Support purchases do not create a restorable feature or entitlement.

Other connections

Apart from the Google services above, the app goes online only in these cases:

  • When you open Privacy policy in Settings, the app downloads this page from GitHub Pages (progressevolvedapp.github.io), which, like any website, receives your IP address and browser details. No app data is sent. The app keeps the last copy so you can read it offline.
  • In the version from Google Play, the app asks the Google Play app whether an update is available when you open it. If you accept an update, Google Play downloads it.
  • Links to our support page, the feedback form, and other websites open in your browser.

Progress Evolved has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. If you let your phone share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give us crash reports showing the device model, Android version, app version, and where the app failed, and Android System WebView, which displays the app, may send Google its own usage and crash statistics.

Tester invite requests

If you request an invite to the Android closed test, the Tally form collects the Google Play email address you provide and, if supplied, your name. Tally hosts and stores the form submission, and sends Progress Evolved an email notification. We use these details only to review your request, add approved accounts to the Google Play testing list, and contact you about access. They are not displayed publicly or combined with your app data. Contact us to correct or delete an invite request.

Feedback and support

If you choose to submit feedback through the Tally form, Tally receives the answers and optional contact email you enter, any files you attach, and the connection details any website receives, such as your IP address. After you choose Save image and open feedback form, Progress Evolved sends the app version and Android API level to Tally in editable prefilled fields. Opening the form does not attach the image or submit a report; you choose whether to attach the saved diagnostic image and submit.

The diagnostic image is a picture of the current app screen with photos and other images hidden, typed text blanked, and all other text hidden except a fixed set of the app's own labels, such as tab names. The image excludes photos, health or weight values, notes, project names, dates, account details, identifiers, and location. It is saved to your phone's gallery in Pictures/Progress Evolved Diagnostics and stays there until you delete it. Its file name shows the app version, release channel, screen, Android API level, and WebView version.

Our private Google review sheet stores submitted answers, attachment file names, and private Tally links to the attached files. A review notification email contains the report title and links. If a report is approved, its answers, attachment names and links, and Tally submission ID are copied to a private GitHub issue, which can display attached images through those links. The files themselves stay in Tally. Tally submissions and review-sheet rows are deleted after 90 days; a private GitHub issue may retain the copied answers, file names, and links after that period. Please do not include personal photos, health information, backups, passwords, or recovery keys.

Our support page also offers GitHub issue forms. Anything you post there is public on GitHub, together with your GitHub username, under GitHub's own terms. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Deletion

Deleting an entry, photo, journal, or project moves it to Trash. Items stay there for 30 days and are then deleted for good; you can restore them or empty Trash sooner.

Under Delete device data in Settings, you can delete all photos, delete all body measurements (entered by you or from Health Connect), or delete all photos and data, which erases every entry, photo, journal, goal, phase, chart event, and import record. These actions take effect at once and skip Trash. Your projects and preferences, including profile height and sex, Health Connect settings, locks, reminders, and automatic backup set-up, are kept. A locked project whose PIN you have forgotten can be erased for good.

Deleting in Progress Evolved does not remove copies outside the app: files you exported or shared, backups in your backup folder, or records written to Health Connect. Deleting a photo in Progress Evolved does not delete the original on your phone. Optional watched-album deletion is a separate, user-initiated Android action and is limited to eligible photos imported from the current watched album. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything Progress Evolved keeps in its own storage on the phone.

Because Progress Evolved has no account server, there is no copy of your app data on our side to delete. Google handles AdMob identifiers and Play purchase records; Progress Evolved does not retain a server-side copy. Purchases remain in Google Play purchase history and permanent purchases can be restored from that record. To delete feedback, an invite request, or an email you sent us, contact us.

Children

Progress Evolved is not designed for children and does not ask your age. Ads are non-personalized and limited to general-audience content for everyone.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. The app shows the latest version when you open Privacy policy in Settings while online.
