Progress Evolved Privacy Policy

Progress Evolved stores body measurements, progress photos, notes, journals, goals, reminders, Health Connect data you choose to import, and purchase and remove-ads state.

The app is local-first. Progress Evolved does not upload tracker history, photos, notes, Health Connect imports, or backup contents to Progress Evolved servers - we do not have any servers. Local app data is stored on your phone and never leaves the device except when you explicitly choose to export, share, or save a backup to a location you control.

Some identifiers and purchase/ad data are processed by Google services when optional ads or purchases are configured: Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. These services do not receive tracker history, progress photos, notes, journals, backup contents, or Health Connect records from Progress Evolved.

Photos

The app can access photos when you take a progress photo, import selected images, share images into Progress Evolved through the Android share sheet, or enable album watch. Album watch uses Android photo-library permission to find new images in chosen albums. You can disable album import in Settings. For a photo imported from the currently watched album, Android 11 and newer may also offer a separate choice to delete the original. Android displays its own confirmation before that source image is deleted; cancelling leaves the Progress Evolved copy intact.

Progress photos are stored encrypted on the device and hidden from the phone gallery. Photos are not uploaded automatically and are not shared with AdMob, Google Play Billing, or Progress Evolved servers.

Samsung Secure Folder uses a separate Android profile. Progress Evolved cannot directly read photos across that profile boundary; you can share photos from inside Secure Folder into Progress Evolved through the Android share sheet.

Health Connect

With your permission, Progress Evolved can read weight, body fat, steps, height, and historical Health Connect data. Optional write-back can write weight, body fat, and steps. You can revoke Health Connect permissions in Android settings.

Health Connect data imported into Progress Evolved is stored locally and is not shared with AdMob, Google Play Billing, or Progress Evolved servers. On Samsung devices, Health Connect data inside Secure Folder is isolated from apps outside that profile; Progress Evolved and Health Connect must be in the same Android profile for import or write-back to work.

Backups And Exports

You can export CSV files, tagged photo bundles, reports, and password-encrypted .pebackup backups. Manual exports are created only when you choose to create them. If you enable automatic backups, the app stores your chosen password in device-local secure storage and creates encrypted backups in the device folder you selected while the app is in use. Exported files leave the phone only if you save or share them to a chosen location such as cloud storage, another app, or external storage. Backup passwords are required to restore backup contents, and the app cannot recover a forgotten password.

Ads And Purchases

The Android app may show banner ads through Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve ads and run consent/fraud-prevention systems. You can remove the banner with a one-time remove-ads purchase. Optional repeatable support purchases grant no features or ad-removal benefit.

Purchases are processed directly by Google Play Billing, which handles the payment flow and store purchase record; Progress Evolved never receives card or payment account details. The app processes the Google Play product ID, purchase state, and purchase token locally only as needed to finish purchases and restore remove-ads ownership. It does not send purchase data to Progress Evolved servers or another billing provider. Support purchases do not create a restorable feature or entitlement.

Deletion

You can delete photos, entries, Health-synced values, or all app data from Settings. Deleted entries and photos may remain in the app trash temporarily before permanent deletion.

Deleting a photo from Progress Evolved alone does not delete the original device or gallery image. Optional watched-album deletion is a separate, user-initiated Android action and is limited to eligible photos imported from the current watched album.

Because Progress Evolved has no account server, deleting local app data deletes the tracker data stored by the app on the device. Google handles advertising identifiers and Play purchase records: you can reset the Android advertising ID in device settings, while purchases remain in Google Play purchase history and permanent purchases can be restored from that record.
